Second Voice

Coercion-aware payment interruptor for UPI

For judges

Three screens, no login. Open them side by side.

  1. The customer's phone. Pick Digital arrest and tap Pay. Then try Normal payment.
  2. Bank A fraud console. The payer bank: cases, signals, transcript, summary.
  3. Bank B console. The receiving bank: its own account scores, the shared band, held credits.

Device signals (active call, screen-share app, payee age) are toggles in the mock app. Registry lookups are stubbed. Everything else is live: Cloud Run, Firestore, Gemini on Vertex AI.
